A group workout challenge that runs itself, inside LINE

Yes Cal is a bot for LINE group chats. Send a screenshot from your watch or fitness app and it reads the numbers and checks you in. Every night at 22:00 it announces who has not worked out yet.

📸 Check in from a screenshot

Send your watch or app summary screen. The bot reads duration, distance and calories — no typing needed.

⏰ A nudge at 22:00

Once a day the bot @-mentions everyone who has not logged a workout, so the reminder actually lands.

🏆 Streaks and standings

Days active out of the last seven, current streak, and lifetime total for each member.

🔒 One group cannot see another

Every group gets its own stats page behind its own secret link. Groups never see each other's data.

Numbers required

A challenge only works if everyone plays it straight, so Yes Cal does not accept vague check-ins. A gym photo, a picture of your shoes, a selfie, or simply typing "worked out" does not count.

It has to be a results screen with real figures, or text with an amount — ran 5 km, weights 1 hr, 50 push-ups. The bot never estimates duration or calories on your behalf; it only shows what it could actually read.

Getting started

  1. Invite Yes Cal into your LINE group
  2. Type ออกกำลังกาย to switch the group into challenge mode
  3. Everyone types เข้าร่วม once to join
  4. Finish a workout, send the screenshot

Connect a wearable

If you wear a WHOOP or a Fitbit you can connect the account to Yes Cal so check-ins happen automatically, without sending a screenshot, and so the group can compare sleep and recovery. Connecting is entirely optional and can be revoked at any time — see the Privacy Policy for exactly what is read and stored.

Yes Cal also counts calories

A second mode logs meals from text or photos, works out daily calorie and protein targets, and suggests dishes that fit what is left of the day.

Yes Cal is a personal project used by a small circle of friends and family. It is free and carries no advertising.
